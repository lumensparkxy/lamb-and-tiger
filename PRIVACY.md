# Privacy Policy — Lamb and Tiger

Last updated: **19 October 2026**

This policy describes the current data practices of **Lamb and Tiger**, the Android board game with application ID `com.maswadkar.lambandtiger`. For privacy questions, contact **[maswadkar@gmail.com](mailto:maswadkar@gmail.com)**.

## Saved games and preferences

The app stores saved games and preferences on your device so that you can continue playing and retain your settings. Nearby play also stores a locally generated player identifier and connection and match information needed to reconnect with the same opponent. You do not need to create an app account.

The computer opponent calculates its moves on your device. Your Solo game is not sent to a cloud AI service to calculate those moves. The current app does not include a custom analytics or advertising SDK.

## Playing with a nearby friend

When you choose to host or join a nearby game, the app uses **Google Play services Nearby Connections**. It exchanges connection and game information with the other device, including player and match identifiers, game actions, and saved-state information needed to keep the match consistent. Compare the displayed connection code with your friend before accepting a connection.

Nearby play uses Bluetooth and Wi-Fi permissions. On some older Android versions, Android also requires location permissions for nearby discovery. The game's code does not request geographic coordinates. These permissions are requested when you choose **Host Game** or **Join Game**; Solo play does not require them.

Google documents that the Nearby SDK collects usage diagnostics, subject to the device's **Usage & diagnostics** setting. These diagnostics include connection-performance metrics and device information such as device model, country, software build version and application package name. This SDK behavior is distinct from the game's locally stored save data.

For details about Google's handling of this information and the available controls, see:

- [Nearby Connections and data collection](https://developers.google.com/nearby/connections/overview#data_collection)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## Advertising

**The current version does not display advertisements and does not include the Google Mobile Ads SDK.**

We may add Google advertising in a future version. Before introducing it, we will update this policy and the app's disclosures to describe the actual advertising services and data practices, and provide the privacy choices and age-appropriate handling required for that version. This statement is not consent to future advertising-related data collection.

## Retention and removal of local information

Game data and preferences remain on your device until removed or replaced through the app's supported operations or Android's app-storage controls. The app disables Android backup for its application data.

You can remove the app's local saved games, preferences and local Nearby identity through **Android Settings** by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. This removes your ability to resume those local saves.

Removing information from your phone does not remove a friend's independent saved match or information already handled by Google under its own services and policies. Google's policies govern its diagnostic-data retention and controls.

## Children and families

Lamb and Tiger is intended for children and adults. The current app does not offer an app account, advertising, freeform chat or photo sharing. Nearby mode exchanges the information required for a game with the other player, as described above.

Parents and guardians can contact **[maswadkar@gmail.com](mailto:maswadkar@gmail.com)** with privacy questions, including concerns about their child's use of the app.
